# Derayah Financial – Equity Research

This repository presents our team project on Derayah Financial as part of the CFA Institute Research Challenge at King Abdulaziz University.

## About the Project

Our team conducted an equity research analysis of Derayah Financial, covering the company's business model, industry and competitive positioning, financial performance, valuation, investment risks, and ESG analysis.

## My Contribution

My main focus was **Financial Analysis & Valuation**, including:

- Financial statement analysis
- Revenue and profitability analysis
- Financial ratios and performance trends
- Financial forecasting
- Dividend Discount Model (DDM)
- P/B and P/E valuation
- Scenario and sensitivity analysis
- Analysis of key financial drivers
